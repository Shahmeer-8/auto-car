Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to questions you may have about contacting us and our process.

How quickly will I receive a response?

We respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days. For urgent matters, please call us directly or use our live chat feature.

Do you speak English?

Yes! Our entire export team is fluent in English. We also have staff who speak Arabic, French, and Swahili to better serve our international clients.

Can I visit your facility in person?

Absolutely! We welcome visitors to our Koshigaya office. Please schedule an appointment in advance so we can arrange a proper tour and have a specialist available.

What information should I include in my inquiry?

Tell us your budget, preferred vehicle type, destination country, and timeline. The more details you provide, the better we can assist you.

Do you provide quotes via email?

Yes, we provide detailed quotes including vehicle price, shipping costs, insurance, and all documentation fees. We ensure complete transparency with no hidden charges.

Can I WhatsApp you instead?

Yes! You can reach us on WhatsApp at +923403024197. We find it's often the fastest way to share vehicle photos and answer questions.
