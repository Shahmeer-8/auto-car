Who We Are

A Decade of Excellence
in Japanese Automotive
Exports.

AutoFlex was born in 2014 from a simple conviction — that buyers worldwide deserve access to Japan's exceptional used car market without the uncertainty, the hidden costs, or the unreliable middlemen that plagued the industry.

Operating from our headquarters in Koshigaya, Saitama, Japan, we immerse ourselves daily in Japan's world-class auction ecosystem — hand-selecting vehicles with Grade 4.0 and above ratings, verified mileage, and clean histories that meet our exacting standards before a single listing goes live.

A decade later, we have served over 8,000 satisfied buyers across 50+ countries, built a team of dedicated export specialists, and earned a reputation that speaks for itself — one delivered car at a time.
